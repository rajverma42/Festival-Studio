Get the Festival Studio app

Festival Studio installs straight from this page. There is no app store, no download queue and no account — the same site you are reading becomes an app with an icon on your home screen, a full-screen window, and everything still working when you are offline.

Tap the button above. If your browser does not offer it, follow the steps for your device below.

Why install it

Works offline

After your first visit the editor, templates, stickers and wishes are stored on your device, so you can finish a post with no signal at all.

Opens like an app

A home screen icon and a full-screen window, with no address bar eating space on a small phone.

Nothing to sign up for

No account, no email, no permissions to accept. Your photos never leave your device.

Barely any space

The whole app is a few megabytes, a fraction of a typical store download.

Android — Chrome, Edge or Brave

Tap Install above

Your browser shows an “Install app” or “Add to Home screen” sheet. Confirm it.

Or use the browser menu

Tap the three dots in the top-right corner, then “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.

Open it from your home screen

The Festival Studio icon now sits with your other apps and opens full screen.

iPhone and iPad — Safari

Open this page in Safari

Installing only works from Safari on iOS. Chrome and other browsers cannot add it.

Tap the Share button

The square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.

Choose “Add to Home Screen”

Scroll the share sheet until you see it, then tap Add. The icon appears on your home screen.

Windows, Mac and Linux

Use Chrome or Edge

Look for the small install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar.

Or the browser menu

Open the three-dot menu and choose “Install Festival Studio”.

It opens in its own window

No tabs, no address bar — and it stays available offline.

Is it on the Play Store or App Store?

No, and it does not need to be. Festival Studio is a Progressive Web App, so it installs directly from this page. That means no store review, no APK file to sideload, no “unknown sources” warning, and updates arrive the moment you open it — you never have to download a new version by hand.

What works offline

The post maker, GIF maker, status maker, every template, all the stickers and the whole wishes collection work with no connection. Only the festival calendar needs the internet the first time it loads.
