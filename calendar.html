Indian festival calendar

Dates with a live countdown, so you can prepare your posts before the rush. Tap any festival to open its post maker.

AD SLOTcalendar-top
Most Indian festivals follow lunar calendars, so their dates change every year. Dates marked approx depend on moon sighting or regional panchang — please confirm locally. Dates are configured in js/festivals.js under FS.FESTIVAL_DATES; add a new year block there once a year to keep this calendar accurate.
