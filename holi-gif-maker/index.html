Free Holi GIF maker

The festival of colours — gulal, water balloons, gujiya and the loudest music of the year. Animate a Holi greeting with sparkles, fireworks, confetti or a diya glow, then download a real .gif file — encoded by your own device, with no upload and no watermark.

Tips for a good Holi GIF

  • Keep the message short — a GIF loops, so two lines read better than five.
  • 10–12 frames per second looks smooth and keeps the file small enough for WhatsApp.
  • Use the 260 px size on older phones; use 540 px for Instagram.
  • Need a still image instead? Use the Holi post maker, or copy a line from Holi wishes.
